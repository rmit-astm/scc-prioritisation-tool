How to use this prototype

Check whether the modelled priorities make practical sense

Questions for practitioners

  • Are the selected gaps genuinely missing or inadequate, or have they already been built or funded?
  • Could the proposed treatment be delivered at the assumed cost?
  • Are important corridors or existing facilities missing from the underlying network?
  • Does the order in which projects enter as funding grows look credible?
  • Where might intersections, bridges, parking or road-space constraints materially change delivery?

What the dashboard represents

Each point is a solved portfolio from a trip-based optimisation model. Benefits are out-of-sample 30-year present values in 2025 Australian dollars. “Use available funding” explores the strongest program within each funding ceiling. “Find preferred scale” can stop spending when additional projects reduce the risk-adjusted net value.

The portfolios are best-found solutions with valid but loose global bounds; they are not proven global optima. Funding levels are optimised independently and should not be interpreted as a staged construction sequence.

Important limitation

These are research outputs, not funded projects, engineering designs or recommendations for construction. Locations inherit uncertainty in SCC matching, current facility classification, cycling routes, uptake and project cost. The low-complexity case is an optimistic bound and is not known to be feasible at every site.
